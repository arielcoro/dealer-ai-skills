# Dealer AI Skills

**AI agent skills for franchise and independent car dealers.** SEO, AEO, GEO, BDC, F&I operations, fixed ops, equity mining, customer lifecycle, and growth engineering. Works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Gemini CLI.

By **[Ariel Coro](https://arielcoro.com)**. Founder of [Dealer Growth Hackers](https://dealergrowthhackers.com). Publisher of [Dealer AI Guy](https://www.dealeraiguy.com). Marketplace at [dealeraiskills.com](https://dealeraiskills.com).

## What this is

A curated collection of 15 installable AI agent skills designed specifically for car dealer retail operations. Each skill encapsulates a workflow that dealers, agencies serving dealers, or technical buyers at dealer groups need to run repeatedly.

Skills here are **vendor-neutral**. They name the operational problem and the steps to solve it. They do not promote a specific DMS, CRM, ad platform, or AI vendor.

Skills are **MIT-licensed** and free to install. They link back to free Dealer AI Guy tools at [dealeraiguy.com/tools](https://www.dealeraiguy.com/tools/) and the paid Dealer Growth Hackers consulting practice at [dealeraiguy.com/consulting](https://www.dealeraiguy.com/consulting/).

## Available skills (15)

### Strategy and planning

#### [dealer-search-strategy](./skills/dealer-search-strategy)
Generate a comprehensive search strategy document covering **SEO** (Search Engine Optimization), **AEO** (Answer Engine Optimization), and **GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization). Clarifies the difference between the three. Produces a unified 4-pillar strategy with an integrated 90-day roadmap covering technical foundation, traditional Google ranking, AI engine citation (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot), and cross-channel reputation.

#### [dealer-ai-readiness-audit](./skills/dealer-ai-readiness-audit)
75-point AI readiness audit across 17 sections of dealership operations. Scores each item Green / Yellow / Red, computes a total out of 150, and places the store in one of four bands (AI-ready, Pilot-ready, Foundation first, Rebuild) with a sequenced 30/60/90 day remediation roadmap. Use before buying any AI tool.

#### [dealer-store-positioning](./skills/dealer-store-positioning)
Foundational positioning brief plus marketing-team-ready content kit. Target customer, value proposition, differentiation, voice and tone, brand promise, "we are / we are not" boundaries, boilerplate language in 3 lengths, tagline options, social media bios, email signatures by role, and pre-approved language guidelines. Used as input by every other marketing skill in the marketplace.

### AI visibility / AEO / GEO

#### [dealer-aeo-audit](./skills/dealer-aeo-audit)
30-check, 10-dimension audit covering AI crawler access, llms.txt, schema markup, entity reinforcement, conversational content, third-party citations, reviews, live AI engine spot-checks, and technical foundation. Finds the specific gaps stopping the dealer from being cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot.

#### [dealer-ai-visibility](./skills/dealer-ai-visibility)
Monitor what AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Google AI Mode) are actually saying about a dealership. Runs a 25 to 30 query taxonomy with bilingual variants, buckets cited sources into 7 categories (OEM, third-party listings, reviews, forums, local press, dealer-owned, other), and computes citation share against named competitor rooftops. Branded at [dealeraivisibility.com](https://dealeraivisibility.com).

#### [dealer-llms-txt-generator](./skills/dealer-llms-txt-generator)
Generate a curated `llms.txt` file for a car dealership website. Produces a paste-ready file optimized for AI engine citation. Strict no-URL-fabrication rule. Supports single rooftops, dealer groups, and bilingual dealers.

#### [dealer-aeo-content-brief](./skills/dealer-aeo-content-brief)
Generate AEO/GEO content briefs for dealer pages or blog posts. Outputs a complete brief with target query, page type, opening 100 words verbatim, section outline, required Q&A with AI extraction sentences, schema spec, and image specs. Includes seven dealer-specific page-type patterns: model guide, service guide, buying help, comparison, financing guide, local landing page, and general blog.

### Technical foundation

#### [dealer-site-score](./skills/dealer-site-score)
The dealer website grader. 100-point evaluation across 10 dimensions: performance (Core Web Vitals), hosting, crawlability, security, schema, mobile, accessibility, third-party tag bloat, conversion elements, and dealer-specific signals. Produces a letter grade and prioritized fix list. The agent-installable version of [dealersitescore.com](https://dealersitescore.com).

### Inventory and merchandising

#### [dealer-vdp-merchandising-review](./skills/dealer-vdp-merchandising-review)
Audit a sample of Vehicle Detail Pages for merchandising quality. 10 dimensions: photo coverage and quality, description quality, pricing transparency, vehicle data completeness, schema markup, CTAs, trust signals, cross-sell elements, mobile UX, and OEM compliance. Brand-aware: applies OEM-specific standards for GM, Ford, Stellantis, Toyota, Honda, Hyundai/Kia, VW/Audi, and Subaru.

### Competitive content

#### [dealer-comparison-page-builder](./skills/dealer-comparison-page-builder)
Build one in-depth comparison page between your dealership and a single named competitor. 10 to 15 dimension comparison framework, fair-play rules (the competitor must come out ahead on at least one dimension for credibility), schema markup spec, and a sales floor talk track for when customers are cross-shopping. Optimized for AEO/GEO citation and Google ranking on "Dealer A vs Dealer B" queries.

### Sales floor honesty

#### [dealer-call-transcript-classifier](./skills/dealer-call-transcript-classifier)
Classify dealer call transcripts into Sales / Service / Parts / Finance / Other with a **strict honesty rule** that never inflates the Sales count. Auto-detects input format (single transcript, batch paste, CallRail or CRM export). For Sales-classified calls, extracts vehicle of interest, lead source, and whether contact was captured. If there are 2 sales calls out of 69 reviewed, the report says 2 out of 69. No rounding up.

### Customer lifecycle

#### [dealer-new-customer-onboarding](./skills/dealer-new-customer-onboarding)
Design a comprehensive 90-day post-sale customer onboarding program. Multi-channel (email + SMS + in-person + optional ringless voicemail) covering delivery through first service intro. Built around the critical sales-to-service handoff that determines long-term service retention. Includes OEM-specific connected services activation flows (OnStar, FordPass, Uconnect, etc.).

#### [dealer-email-flows](./skills/dealer-email-flows)
17 dealer-specific lifecycle email flows covering the full customer relationship from purchase through year 5+: service reminders, declined recovery, lease maturity, equity check-in, anniversary, OEM event invite, lapsed-service revival, recall outreach, birthday, holiday, win-back, referral, test-drive follow-up, quote follow-up, service appointment confirmation, post-service NPS, and delivery welcome. TCPA-compliant with opt-in gates, suppression checks, and frequency caps.

### Demand generation

#### [dealer-equity-mining-campaign-builder](./skills/dealer-equity-mining-campaign-builder)
Build a complete equity mining campaign: blueprint (cadence, channels, gates, KPIs) plus messaging templates (SMS, email, ringless voicemail) across the proven 3-channel cadence. Six standard personas: lease returner, equity-positive trade-up, service-to-sales (S2S), OEM-program eligible, upside-down upgrader, dormant reactivation. Compliance guardrails baked into every touch.

### Reputation

#### [dealer-customer-sentiment-analyzer](./skills/dealer-customer-sentiment-analyzer)
Analyze dealer customer reviews honestly. Reads the text, not just the star rating. Department-level sentiment (sales, service, F&I, parts), top recurring themes with quoted evidence, named-staff mentions, trend analysis when dates are available, and a prioritized list of reviews that warrant a dealer response. Auto-detects input format (single review, batch paste, CSV export from Google, DealerRater, Cars.com, Yelp, BBB, Facebook).

## Install

### Option 1: Claude Code plugin marketplace (recommended)

Inside Claude Code:

```bash
/plugin marketplace add arielcoro/dealer-ai-skills
/plugin install [skill-name]@dealer-ai-skills
```

Replace `[skill-name]` with the skill you want. Examples:

```bash
/plugin install dealer-ai-readiness-audit@dealer-ai-skills
/plugin install dealer-aeo-audit@dealer-ai-skills
/plugin install dealer-search-strategy@dealer-ai-skills
```

Each skill activates automatically when relevant phrases come up in conversation.

### Option 2: Universal install via npx skills

```bash
npx skills add arielcoro/dealer-ai-skills
```

Works across Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, and any agent that supports the [Agent Skills spec](https://agentskills.io).

### Option 3: Direct skill copy

```bash
git clone https://github.com/arielcoro/dealer-ai-skills.git
cp -r dealer-ai-skills/skills/[skill-name] ~/.claude/skills/
```

Drop the skill folder into any agent's skills directory.

### Option 4: Plain English

Tell your agent things like:

- "Run the Dealer AI Readiness Audit on my store."
- "Audit my dealer site for AEO."
- "Generate llms.txt for my GMC dealership."
- "What is ChatGPT saying about my dealership?"
- "Build a comparison page: Howard Bentley vs Birmingham Buick."
- "Classify these call transcripts and tell me how many sales calls."
- "Design our 90-day new customer onboarding program."
- "Build the lifecycle email flows for our dealership."

The right skill activates from natural language once installed.

## How the skills work together

The 15 skills are designed as a coherent toolkit, not a list of independent tools. Several skills reference each other and produce outputs that feed other skills.

**Foundational flow:**

`dealer-store-positioning` → produces voice, target customer, value props → used as input by `dealer-aeo-content-brief`, `dealer-email-flows`, `dealer-new-customer-onboarding`, `dealer-comparison-page-builder`.

**AI visibility flow:**

`dealer-aeo-audit` (find gaps) → `dealer-llms-txt-generator` (close infrastructure gap) → `dealer-aeo-content-brief` (close content gap) → `dealer-ai-visibility` (measure live result) → repeat quarterly.

**Customer lifecycle flow:**

Sale → `dealer-new-customer-onboarding` (first 90 days) → `dealer-email-flows` (year 1+) → `dealer-equity-mining-campaign-builder` (when ready to upgrade) → repeat.

**Diagnostic flow:**

`dealer-ai-readiness-audit` (operational baseline) + `dealer-site-score` (technical baseline) + `dealer-aeo-audit` (AI visibility baseline) → `dealer-search-strategy` (synthesizes into integrated plan).

## Why this exists

There is a reason most dealer AI projects underperform. It is not the AI. It is the dealership. A store with a messy CRM, a slow website, no governance, and a BDC running on hope cannot install a chatbot and suddenly become a tech company. The AI exposes the cracks faster.

This marketplace exists to make rigorous, vendor-neutral diagnostics and operational workflows installable inside the agent the dealer already uses. The frameworks are the same ones [Ariel Coro](https://arielcoro.com) runs on prospect and client stores at [Dealer Growth Hackers](https://dealergrowthhackers.com) before any AI engagement.
