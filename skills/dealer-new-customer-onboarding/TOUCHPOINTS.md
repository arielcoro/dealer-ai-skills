# Dealer New Customer Onboarding — Touchpoint Detail

The day-by-day, channel-by-channel touchpoint detail. SKILL.md and REFERENCE.md reference this file. Use these as the starting templates; customize per dealer voice and brand portfolio.

Merge tokens used throughout: `{first_name}`, `{vehicle_year}`, `{vehicle_make}`, `{vehicle_model}`, `{salesperson_name}`, `{service_advisor_name}`, `{dealership_name}`, `{phone_number}`, `{service_phone}`, `{calendly_link}`, `{review_link}`, `{app_name}`.

## Day 0 — Delivery touchpoints (in-person)

### Touchpoint 0A: Vehicle delivery walkaround

**Owner:** Salesperson (or designated delivery coordinator)
**Channel:** In-person
**Duration:** 30 to 45 minutes
**Trigger:** Customer arrives for delivery

**SOP:**

1. Welcome customer, hand keys ceremoniously.
2. Walkaround of vehicle exterior (note any pre-existing items, walk-through care basics).
3. Interior orientation: starting, climate, infotainment, key fob features.
4. OEM connected services activation (next touchpoint).
5. Trunk/cargo features, safety system overview.
6. Service introduction (next touchpoint).
7. Final paperwork review and customer questions.

**Success criteria:** Customer leaves with vehicle, completed activation, named service advisor introduction, and clear sense of what happens next.

### Touchpoint 0B: OEM connected services activation

**Owner:** Salesperson (or designated tech-savvy team member)
**Channel:** In-person
**Duration:** 10 to 15 minutes
**Trigger:** Part of delivery walkaround

**SOP (GM example — adjust per brand):**

1. Have customer install `{app_name}` on their phone.
2. Walk through OnStar enrollment in vehicle.
3. Pair phone to vehicle and app.
4. Show customer: remote start, vehicle diagnostics, alert customization.
5. Confirm the trial period and what happens after.

**Success criteria:** App installed, account created, vehicle paired, customer can demonstrate one app feature unassisted.

### Touchpoint 0C: Service advisor introduction

**Owner:** Salesperson + named service advisor
**Channel:** In-person
**Duration:** 5 to 10 minutes
**Trigger:** End of delivery walkaround

**SOP:**

1. Salesperson walks customer to service drive (or invites service advisor to sales area).
2. Salesperson introduces by name: "{first_name}, this is {service_advisor_name}, our [Brand] master service advisor. They'll be your go-to for service questions."
3. Service advisor hands business card with direct phone.
4. Service advisor offers brief tour: "Let me show you where to park for service, where loaners are, where the customer waiting area is."
5. Optional: schedule a "service intro visit" within 14 days if customer wants to drop by.

**Success criteria:** Customer has the named service advisor's business card and direct phone. Customer knows where to go for service.

{salesperson_name}
{dealership_name}

### Touchpoint 3B: Optional service intro visit (in-person)

**Owner:** Service advisor
**Channel:** In-person
**Duration:** 10 to 15 minutes
**Trigger:** Customer responds to email invitation OR happens to be in the area

**SOP:**

1. Service advisor greets customer by name.
2. Quick tour: service drive parking, loaner office, customer waiting area, restrooms.
3. Light explanation: "When you come for your first service in [X] weeks, here's what to expect."
4. Optional: schedule first service appointment if customer is ready.
5. Thank customer for stopping by.

**Success criteria:** Customer has been physically in the service area and knows exactly what to expect.

## Day 61 — First service experience (in-person)

### Touchpoint 9: First service visit

**Owner:** Service advisor + service team
**Channel:** In-person
**Duration:** 60 to 90 minutes typical
**Trigger:** Customer arrives for first service appointment

**SOP:**

1. Service advisor greets customer by name, references delivery introduction.
2. Confirm services to be performed; explain timing.
3. Offer waiting area amenities (coffee, Wi-Fi, etc.) or loaner.
4. Throughout service: status updates by SMS if customer prefers.
5. At completion: service advisor walks customer through what was done.
6. Address any customer questions or concerns.
7. Schedule next service if appropriate (typically 5,000 to 7,500 miles out).
8. Hand customer a follow-up card with service advisor's direct line again.
9. Thank customer warmly.

**Success criteria:** Customer leaves having had a positive in-person experience with a named human (the service advisor). Next service is on the calendar or planned.

## How to customize per dealer

The templates above are starting points. Customize per:

- **Voice.** Use the voice from dealer-store-positioning output. Some dealers are warmer, some more direct, some more formal.
- **Brand portfolio.** Different OEMs have different app names, different connected services platforms. Replace `{app_name}` and related tokens appropriately.
- **Salesperson culture.** Some dealerships have strong personal salesperson relationships (use the salesperson's name throughout). Others have a more BDC-driven model (use BDC or service advisor instead).
- **Service capacity.** If service is at capacity, slot suggestion windows can be tighter.
- **Bilingual.** Produce Spanish versions for bilingual markets.

When in doubt, the rule is: simpler and more honest beats clever and copy-heavy. The goal is the long-term service relationship, not the cleverness of any one touchpoint.
