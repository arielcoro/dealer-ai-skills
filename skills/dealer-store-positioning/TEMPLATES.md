# Dealer Store Positioning — Content Kit Templates

The marketing-team-ready templates that get filled in based on the positioning brief. SKILL.md references this file. Each template has a fill-in skeleton plus quality rules.

---

## Boilerplate language

Produced in three lengths. Each length has a specific use case.

### 25-word boilerplate

**Use cases:** Footers, captions, brief mentions, social media post sign-offs, image alt text.

**Template:**

```
[Dealership Name] is a [years]-year-old [brand portfolio] dealer in [City, State] serving [target customer description] with [primary differentiator].
```

**Example:**

> Howard Bentley Buick GMC is a family-owned franchise GMC and Buick dealer in Albertville, Alabama serving north Alabama families with top-five national GMC delivery volume and bilingual service.

**Quality rules:**
- Under 30 words total
- Includes dealership name, brand portfolio, location
- Includes one differentiator
- Reads as a single complete sentence

### 50-word boilerplate

**Use cases:** Press releases, social media bios, OEM submissions, partnership documents.

**Template:**

```
[Dealership Name] is a [years]-year-old franchise [brand portfolio] dealer in [City, State]. Owned by [ownership]. Recognized as [most recent OEM award or notable recognition]. [Service capability summary]. Serving [target customer description] with [3 differentiators in a single sentence].
```

**Example:**

> Howard Bentley Buick GMC is a 40-year-old franchise GMC and Buick dealer in Albertville, Alabama, family-owned and recognized as a top-five national GMC dealer in 2025 with 3,955 deliveries. Factory-trained GMC and Buick service available six days a week. Serving north Alabama families with no-pressure sales, transparent F&I, and Spanish-speaking staff across departments.

**Quality rules:**
- 45 to 60 words
- Three sentences maximum
- Includes ownership and history
- Includes most recent OEM recognition with date
- Includes 3 differentiators

### 100-word boilerplate

**Use cases:** Blog footers, longer mentions, partnership documents, About page openers, OEM-submitted content.

**Template:**

```
[Dealership Name] is a [years]-year-old franchise [brand portfolio] dealer in [City, State], owned by [ownership]. Founded in [year], the dealership has grown to [size/capacity signal] serving [target customer segments] across [service area].

In [most recent year], [Dealership Name] was recognized as [OEM award]. The dealership maintains [number] OEM-certified technicians, a six-day-a-week service drive, and a no-pressure sales process designed around [primary differentiator].

[Dealership Name] is [bilingual / community involvement signal] and serves [target customer description] with [primary value proposition restated in 15-20 words].
```

**Example:**

> Howard Bentley Buick GMC is a 40-year-old franchise GMC and Buick dealer in Albertville, Alabama, family-owned by the Bentley family. Founded in 1985, the dealership has grown to serve north Alabama families across Marshall, Etowah, DeKalb, and Madison counties.
>
> In 2025, Howard Bentley was recognized as a top-five national GMC dealer with 3,955 deliveries. The dealership maintains GMC and Buick master technician certifications, a six-day-a-week service drive, and a no-pressure sales process designed around transparent pricing and respect for the customer's time.
>
> Howard Bentley is bilingual across sales, service, and F&I, and serves north Alabama families with the discipline of a top-five national store and the relationships of a family-owned dealership.

**Quality rules:**
- 90 to 120 words
- Three paragraphs
- Includes founding year, ownership, service area
- Includes most recent OEM recognition
- Includes operational specifics (technician count, service availability)
- Ends with the value proposition restated

## Tagline options

Produce 5 to 8 tagline options. For each: the tagline, the rationale, and the contexts where it works or does not work.

**Format for each option:**

```
TAGLINE: [The actual tagline]
RATIONALE: [Why this tagline; what positioning element it captures]
WORKS WELL FOR: [Specific contexts, channels, audiences]
DOES NOT WORK FOR: [Contexts where this tagline would fall flat or feel off-brand]
```

**Quality rules for taglines:**

- 3 to 7 words ideal; up to 10 acceptable.
- Specific verbs and concrete nouns. Avoid abstract claims.
- True to the differentiation, not generic.
- One angle per tagline; do not stack multiple claims in one tagline.
- Pronounceable. The dealer principal must be able to say it without cringing.
- Avoid rhyme, alliteration, or wordplay unless it specifically supports the brand voice.

**Tagline angles to produce variants across:**

1. **Differentiation-led** — names the primary differentiation directly.
2. **Customer-promise-led** — names what the customer gets.
3. **Heritage-led** — names the history or family-owned story.
4. **Community-led** — names the community connection.
5. **Bilingual-friendly** — works in both English and Spanish without translation gymnastics.
6. **Service-led** — emphasizes the service drive.
7. **Trust-led** — emphasizes the transparency or trust differentiator.
8. **Local-pride-led** — names the specific market or region.

Not every dealership needs all 8 angles. Produce the variants that fit the dealership's actual positioning.

## Social media bios

Produce templates for the dealership's official accounts and key personal accounts.

### LinkedIn company page bio

**Template:**

```
[Brand portfolio] dealer in [City, State] | [Years]-year-old [ownership type] | [Most recent OEM recognition] | Serving [target customer description] with [primary differentiator]

[Service capability one-liner]
[Bilingual signal if applicable]

[Website URL]
```

**Quality rules:**
- Under 220 characters for the headline portion
- Uses pipe separators for scannability
- Includes most recent OEM recognition
- Includes website URL

### LinkedIn personal page templates

**Template for dealer principal:**

```
Dealer Principal at [Dealership Name] | [Brand portfolio] in [City] | [Most recent OEM recognition] | Building [target customer description] relationships across [region]

[One-sentence differentiation or philosophy]
```

**Template for GM:**

```
General Manager at [Dealership Name] | [Brand portfolio] in [City] | [Operational responsibility scope] | [One signal of expertise: tenure, certifications, achievements]

[One-sentence operational philosophy]
```

**Template for sales manager and service manager:** Same pattern, role-specific.

### Facebook business page bio

**Template:**

```
[Brand portfolio] dealer in [City, State] serving [target customer description]. [Most recent OEM recognition]. Family-owned since [year]. [Bilingual signal]. [Phone]. [Website].
```

**Quality rules:**
- Under 255 characters
- Plain prose, not pipe-separated
- Includes phone and website

### Instagram bio

**Template:**

```

[City, State]
[One-line value prop]
[One-line specialty if applicable]
[Link]
```

**Quality rules:**
- Under 150 characters
- 4 to 5 short lines
- Voice-appropriate (some dealers use emojis, some do not; respect the voice)

### X bio

**Template:**

```
[Brand portfolio] dealer in [City] | [Differentiator] | Family-owned since [year] | [Phone]
```

**Quality rules:**
- Under 160 characters
- Pipe-separated

### YouTube channel description

## Value proposition variants

Five angles, each tailored to a specific audience or context.

### Sales prospect variant

**Template:**

```
[Dealership Name] is the [brand portfolio] dealer in [City] for [target customer description] who want [primary buying motivation]. We deliver [primary value proposition restated for sales context].
```

**Tone:** Confident, direct, customer-focused. No hard-sell language.

### Service customer variant

**Template:**

```
At [Dealership Name], your [brand] service is handled by [OEM-certified technician count] [brand]-certified master technicians, Monday through [day] from [hours]. We deliver [service-specific value proposition].
```

**Tone:** Practical, capability-focused. Specifics over slogans.

### Finance customer variant

**Template:**

```
[Dealership Name] partners with [lender count] lenders including [3 named lenders] to find financing for buyers across the credit spectrum. We deliver [finance-specific value proposition: typically transparency or specific capability].
```

**Tone:** Reassuring, factual. Avoid pressure language.

### Community member variant

**Template:**

```
[Dealership Name] has been part of [community name] for [years]. We support [community involvement specifics]. We employ [staff size signal] and serve [customer relationship signal]. We deliver [community-facing value proposition].
```

**Tone:** Warm, rooted, locally specific.

### OEM and partner variant

**Template:**

```
[Dealership Name] is a [most recent OEM award level] [brand] dealer in [region], delivering [annual volume signal] units annually with [specialization or capability signal]. [Most recent metric of significance]. Owned by [ownership type] and operating from [facility description].
```

**Tone:** Professional, metric-driven, B2B-appropriate.

## Things to say

Produce a list of 10 to 15 pre-approved phrases, claims, and framings the marketing team can use freely. Each entry is concrete and on-brand.

**Examples (customize per dealership):**

- "Top-five national GMC dealer in 2025 with 3,955 deliveries"
- "GMC and Buick master technicians"
- "Family-owned since 1985"
- "Bilingual sales, service, and F&I staff"
- "No-pressure sales process"
- "Same-day service for routine maintenance"
- "GMC Loyalty Owner Bonus eligible"
- "Trade-in value walkthroughs with every customer"
- "Saturday service hours from 8 AM to 2 PM"
- "North Alabama's [specific superlative the dealership can defend]"

**Quality rules:**
- Each item is specific, not generic
- Each item is true today (not aspirational)
- Each item has supporting evidence (OEM ranking, certifications, hours, etc.)
- No unverifiable superlatives

## Things to avoid

Produce a list of 10 to 15 phrases, claims, and framings the marketing team should not use. Includes both off-brand language and legally risky claims.

**Categories:**

### Unsubstantiated superlatives
- "Best dealer in [region]"
- "Number one for service"
- "Cheapest prices in town"
- "Largest selection anywhere"

### Generic dealer marketing language
- "Where deals are made"
- "Drive happy"
- "Family of brands you can trust"
- "Where customer service comes first"

### Off-brand voice (specific to the dealership)
- [Specific phrases that conflict with the documented voice]
- [Specific tones that the dealership has explicitly chosen against]

### Pressure language
- "Limited time only"
- "Don't miss out"
- "Once-in-a-lifetime"
- "Act now"

### Legally risky claims
- Unverified comparisons ("Better than [Competitor]")
- Unverifiable savings ("Save up to $10,000")
- Inflation of OEM programs ("Up to" claims with unrealistic ceilings)
- Implied financing terms not pre-approved

**Quality rules:**
- Each item is specific (a phrase or claim type, not a vague guideline)
- Each item has a brief rationale (why this is off-brand or risky)

## How to deliver the content kit

The content kit gets delivered as the second half of the positioning document. Recommended internal distribution:

- **Master document:** Marketing director holds. All updates flow from here.
- **Sales floor handout:** Boilerplate options, value prop variants, things to say, things to avoid. Used by the BDC and sales managers.
- **Service department handout:** Service-specific value props, things to say. Used by service advisors and the service manager.
- **F&I handout:** Finance-specific value props, things to say, things to avoid (especially around pressure language).
- **Agency partner read-only copy:** Full positioning document plus content kit. Used by external partners producing marketing content.
- **OEM partner copy:** 100-word boilerplate and OEM-facing value prop variant. Submitted to OEM marketing programs.

Re-issue the content kit when the positioning brief is refreshed (typically every 12 to 24 months) or when significant changes occur (ownership change, new brand added, new rooftop opened, major OEM award earned).
