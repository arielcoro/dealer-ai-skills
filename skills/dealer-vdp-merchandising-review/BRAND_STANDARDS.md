# Dealer VDP Merchandising Review — OEM Brand Standards

Brand-specific photo standards, required disclosures, and brand-mandated content for major OEMs in the US market. SKILL.md references this file. Apply the section that matches each brand in the dealer's portfolio.

This file is operational guidance based on commonly applied OEM standards. OEM requirements change. The dealer's brand operations manager or OEM contact should verify the latest requirements for any compliance audit. This document represents what the framework checks against at the time of writing.

---

## How to use this file

When the user confirms the dealership's brand portfolio at the start of the audit, the skill applies the matching section below to Dimension 10 (OEM compliance) of each VDP for that brand. For brands not covered in this file, the skill falls back to brand-neutral checks only.

When a brand appears in the dealer portfolio, apply:
- The photo standards section (Dimension 1 enhancements)
- The required disclosures section (Dimension 10.2)
- The brand-mandated content section (Dimension 10.3)
- The brand-specific flags section

## General Motors (GMC, Buick, Chevrolet, Cadillac)

GM dealers operate under "Family of Brands" merchandising standards. All four brands share core requirements with brand-specific photo and badge variations.

### Photo standards

- Front 3/4 driver side as the primary lead photo.
- Rear 3/4 passenger side as the second photo.
- Interior photos must show the GM-branded infotainment screen (where applicable) in a powered-on state.
- For new vehicles, the brand badge (GMC, Buick, Chevrolet, or Cadillac logo) must be clearly visible in at least one exterior photo.
- For trucks with available trim badges (Denali, AT4, AT4X), the trim badge should appear in at least one close-up shot.

### Required disclosures

- "$X plus tax, title, registration, and dealer documentation fee" disclosure on every priced offer.
- Lease offers must include monthly payment, total down, term, miles per year, residual disclosure language, and "subject to credit approval".
- Incentive disclosures must specify eligibility and deadline (e.g., "GM Loyalty bonus requires current GM ownership; offer ends [date]").
- For Denali, AT4X, and Cadillac trims, MSRP must be displayed; sale price below MSRP must show the savings as a delta.
- Service contracts and OnStar trial period disclosures where applicable.

### Brand-mandated content

- Current OEM warranty terms (3 years / 36,000 miles bumper-to-bumper, 5 years / 60,000 miles powertrain for most brands).
- OnStar trial period information where applicable.
- For Buick: lifetime tire rotation reference where applicable.
- For Cadillac: 4-year / 50,000 mile warranty terms and Cadillac Concierge information.
- For Chevrolet trucks and full-size SUVs: Trailering capability data when sold as towing-capable.

### GM-specific flags

- Flag if a Denali or AT4X VDP does not show the trim badge in photos.
- Flag if a Buick VDP does not include the lifetime tire rotation reference (where applicable).
- Flag if a Chevrolet truck VDP does not include trailering capability data.
- Flag if a Cadillac VDP does not include Cadillac Concierge content.
- Flag if any GM VDP shows MSRP without a clear "savings" delta when a sale price is below MSRP.

## Ford and Lincoln

Ford and Lincoln operate under separate but related brand standards. Ford emphasizes "Built Ford Tough" and SYNC connectivity; Lincoln emphasizes Lincoln Way and concierge services.

### Photo standards

- Front 3/4 driver side as the primary lead photo.
- For trucks (F-150, Super Duty, Maverick, Ranger), bed shot showing tailgate.
- For Lincoln, interior photo must show the dashboard with ambient lighting visible if available.
- Brand badge (Ford oval or Lincoln star) must be clearly visible in at least one exterior shot.

### Required disclosures

- "$X plus tax, title, registration, and dealer documentation fee" disclosure on every priced offer.
- Lease offers must include monthly payment, total down, term, miles per year, residual disclosure, "subject to credit approval".
- Ford Credit and Lincoln AFS incentive disclosures with eligibility and deadline.
- For Ford EV models (Mustang Mach-E, F-150 Lightning, E-Transit), Federal EV tax credit disclosure with applicable qualifications.
- SYNC connected services trial period disclosures.

### Brand-mandated content

- Ford warranty: 3 years / 36,000 miles bumper-to-bumper, 5 years / 60,000 miles powertrain, 5 years / 100,000 miles drivetrain on Super Duty.
- Lincoln warranty: 4 years / 50,000 miles bumper-to-bumper, 6 years / 70,000 miles powertrain.
- For trucks: Trailer Tow capability, payload capacity.
- For Mustang and performance models: Performance specs (horsepower, torque, 0-60).
- For EV models: EPA range estimate, charging speed, home charger compatibility.
- SYNC version and connectivity features.

### Ford and Lincoln-specific flags

- Flag if a Ford truck VDP does not include trailer tow and payload data.
- Flag if a Mustang VDP does not include performance specs.
- Flag if a Ford EV VDP does not include EPA range and Federal tax credit reference.
- Flag if a Lincoln VDP uses Ford-brand template imagery or text.

## Stellantis (Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram, FIAT, Alfa Romeo)

Stellantis operates with brand-specific standards per nameplate. Jeep emphasizes Trail Rated and off-road capability; Ram emphasizes Best-in-Class capability; Dodge emphasizes performance.

### Photo standards

- Front 3/4 driver side as primary.
- For Jeep, at least one photo showing the Trail Rated badge (where applicable).
- For Ram trucks, bed shot showing tailgate and Ram badge.
- For Dodge performance models (Challenger, Charger, Durango SRT), engine bay or performance badge visible in at least one shot.
- Interior photo showing Uconnect infotainment in powered-on state.

### Required disclosures

- "$X plus tax, title, registration, and dealer documentation fee" disclosure on every priced offer.
- Lease offers include monthly payment, total down, term, miles per year, residual, "subject to credit approval".
- Stellantis Financial Services incentive disclosures with eligibility and deadline.
- For Jeep 4xe and Wrangler EV variants: Federal EV tax credit disclosure.
- For Ram trucks with EcoDiesel: emissions and fuel disclosure language.
- Uconnect connected services trial disclosures.

### Brand-mandated content

- Brand warranties (typically 3 years / 36,000 miles bumper-to-bumper, 5 years / 60,000 miles powertrain; Jeep has additional off-road component coverage).
- For Jeep Trail Rated vehicles: Trail Rated badge content (water fording, traction, articulation, ground clearance, maneuverability).
- For Ram: Best-in-Class capability content where applicable (towing, payload, fuel economy).
- For Dodge performance vehicles: Horsepower, torque, 0-60, quarter mile times.
- Uconnect version and infotainment features.

### Stellantis-specific flags

- Flag if a Jeep VDP for a Trail Rated vehicle does not display the Trail Rated content.
- Flag if a Ram VDP does not include towing and payload data for capable trims.
- Flag if a Dodge performance VDP does not include performance specs.
- Flag if a 4xe or EV VDP does not include Federal EV tax credit reference.

## Toyota (Toyota, Lexus)

Toyota and Lexus operate under separate brand programs but share many merchandising principles.

### Photo standards

- Front 3/4 driver side as primary.
- For Toyota trucks (Tundra, Tacoma) and SUVs (4Runner, Sequoia), capability shot showing off-road or towing capability where relevant.
- For Toyota hybrid and EV models (Prius, RAV4 Hybrid, bZ4X), badge or hybrid logo visible.
- Interior photo showing infotainment in powered-on state.
- For Lexus, at least one interior photo showing premium materials (leather, wood, ambient lighting).

### Required disclosures

- "$X plus tax, title, registration, and dealer documentation fee" disclosure.
- Lease offers include monthly payment, total down, term, miles per year, residual, "subject to credit approval".
- Toyota Financial Services and Lexus Financial Services incentive disclosures with eligibility and deadline.
- For bZ4X and other EVs: Federal EV tax credit disclosure with qualifications.
- ToyotaCare and Lexus Service Plus disclosures.

### Brand-mandated content

- Toyota warranty: 3 years / 36,000 miles bumper-to-bumper, 5 years / 60,000 miles powertrain, 8 years / 100,000 miles hybrid battery on hybrid models.
- Lexus warranty: 4 years / 50,000 miles bumper-to-bumper, 6 years / 70,000 miles powertrain.
- For hybrids: Hybrid battery warranty, fuel economy, electric range (PHEV).
- For Lexus: Lexus Service Plus content (complimentary maintenance period).
- ToyotaCare content for new Toyota vehicles (2 years / 25,000 miles of scheduled maintenance).

### Toyota and Lexus-specific flags

- Flag if a Toyota hybrid VDP does not include hybrid battery warranty reference.
- Flag if a Toyota EV VDP does not include EPA range and Federal tax credit reference.
- Flag if a Lexus VDP uses Toyota-brand template imagery or text.
- Flag if a new Toyota VDP does not include ToyotaCare reference.

## Hyundai and Kia (and Genesis)

Hyundai and Kia operate under separate but related brand programs. Genesis is a luxury sub-brand.

### Photo standards

- Front 3/4 driver side as primary.
- For Hyundai and Kia EV models (Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, EV6, EV9), badge or EV logo visible.
- For Genesis, premium materials shots and ambient lighting where applicable.

### Required disclosures

- "$X plus tax, title, registration, and dealer documentation fee".
- Lease offers include monthly payment, total down, term, miles per year, residual, "subject to credit approval".
- Hyundai Motor Finance, Kia Motors Finance, and Genesis Finance incentive disclosures with eligibility and deadline.
- For EV models: Federal EV tax credit disclosure (note: Hyundai and Kia EVs sold but assembled outside North America may have specific tax credit limitations).

### Brand-mandated content

- Hyundai and Kia warranties: 5 years / 60,000 miles bumper-to-bumper, 10 years / 100,000 miles powertrain (long-warranty positioning).
- Genesis warranty: 5 years / 60,000 miles bumper-to-bumper, 10 years / 100,000 miles powertrain.
- For EVs: Battery warranty (typically 10 years / 100,000 miles), EPA range, charging speed.
- Genesis: Concierge content where applicable.

### Hyundai, Kia, and Genesis-specific flags

- Flag if an EV VDP does not include EPA range and Federal tax credit disclosure (with assembly-location note where applicable).
- Flag if a Hyundai or Kia VDP does not include the long-warranty positioning content.
- Flag if a Genesis VDP uses Hyundai or Kia template imagery or text.

## Subaru

Subaru operates under brand-specific standards focused on safety and capability.

### Photo standards

- Front 3/4 driver side as primary.
- For Outback, Forester, Crosstrek: at least one capability photo (off-road, adventure use case).
- Symmetrical AWD badge or all-wheel-drive content visible.

### Required disclosures

- "$X plus tax, title, registration, and dealer documentation fee".
- Lease offers include monthly payment, total down, term, miles per year, residual, "subject to credit approval".
- Subaru Motors Finance incentive disclosures with eligibility and deadline.
- For Solterra EV: Federal EV tax credit disclosure.

### Brand-mandated content

- Subaru warranty: 3 years / 36,000 miles bumper-to-bumper, 5 years / 60,000 miles powertrain.
- Subaru Symmetrical AWD content (standard on most models).
- EyeSight Driver Assist content for equipped models.
- Subaru Love Promise community content where applicable.

### Subaru-specific flags

- Flag if a Subaru VDP does not include Symmetrical AWD reference (on AWD models).
- Flag if an EyeSight-equipped VDP does not include EyeSight content.

## Nissan, Infiniti, Mazda, and other brands

For brands not detailed above (Nissan, Infiniti, Mazda, Mitsubishi, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Volvo, Polestar, Jaguar, Land Rover, Mini, Tesla, Rivian, Lucid, etc.), the skill applies the brand-neutral framework from REFERENCE.md plus general best-practice OEM compliance checks:

- Required disclosure pattern ("$X plus tax, title, registration, dealer doc fee").
- Lease offer pattern (monthly payment, down, term, miles per year, "subject to credit approval").
- EV tax credit disclosure if applicable.
- OEM warranty terms reference.

When the dealer's portfolio includes one of these brands and the user wants brand-specific checks, the skill flags this and recommends the dealer's OEM contact confirm the specific brand standards. The skill does not invent brand-specific requirements.

## How brand standards layer on top of REFERENCE.md

For each VDP audited:

1. Run all 10 dimensions from REFERENCE.md against the brand-neutral standards.
2. For the brand of the vehicle on the VDP, identify the matching section in this file.
3. Apply the photo standards, required disclosures, brand-mandated content, and brand-specific flags from that section.
4. Adjust the Dimension 10 (OEM compliance) score based on the brand-specific findings.

If a VDP shows a brand not covered in this file, default to brand-neutral Dimension 10 scoring and flag the limitation in the report.
