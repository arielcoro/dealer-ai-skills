# Report template

## Verdict
Website, business purpose, target market/language, date, sample size, mode (live or artifact-based), practical conclusion, critical blockers.

## Evidence and coverage
Evidence ID | URL/artifact | retrieval/test method | date/time | observation | limitation.

Category | applicable checks | tested checks | earned points | unknown points | score range | coverage.
Show overall range and whether provisional; give N/A reasons. Do not mix optional connector readiness or observed citation counts into the website score.

## Shopping/customer scenarios (when tested)
Exact prompt | platform/mode | market/session context | date | cited URLs | observed accuracy/omissions | next-action result | evidence ID.
List untested platforms explicitly. Separate same-scenario competitor comparisons from general competitive observations.

## Prioritized fix plan
Priority | affected URL/template | observed symptom | confirmed/suspected cause | fix | owner | acceptance test | evidence ID | confidence.
Group immediate blockers, subsequent data/content corrections, and optional enhancements. Do not assign fixed deadlines without scope information.

## Optional connections
Need/status, advertised versus verified capabilities, tested boundaries, pending authorization or access requirements.

## Validation plan and limitations
Repeat baseline tests after changes; retain dates and contextual differences. Document missing logs, analytics, private feeds, form-delivery tests, and platform access. AI responses vary. Findings are not proof of lost sales or guaranteed future visibility. Public checks do not establish full security or legal compliance.
