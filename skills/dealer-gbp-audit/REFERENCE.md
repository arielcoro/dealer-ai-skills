# Dealer GBP Audit — Framework Reference

10 dimensions. 100 points total. Per-dimension sub-scoring rules. Letter grade bands. Multi-rooftop overlay.

This is the authoritative scoring framework. SKILL.md and CHECKS.md reference this file. Do not paraphrase from memory.

---

## Dimension 1. Primary category accuracy — 12 pts

The primary category is the single highest-leverage local SEO setting on a GBP. It dictates which queries the dealership is eligible to rank for.

### 1.1 Category matches the dealer's primary OEM and sales motion (6 pts)
- 6: Primary category exactly matches the OEM and sales model. Examples: "Buick dealer," "Chevrolet dealer," "Ford dealer," "Toyota dealer," "Honda dealer," "Used car dealer." Multi-brand stores select the largest-volume brand as primary.
- 3: Primary category is correct OEM but lower-specificity (e.g., "Car dealer" instead of "Buick dealer").
- 0: Primary category is wrong, generic, or unrelated (e.g., "Auto repair shop" on a sales rooftop, or "Used car dealer" on a franchise new-car rooftop).

### 1.2 Category not stuffed in the business name (3 pts)
- 3: Business name is the legal entity (e.g., "Howard Bentley Buick GMC"). No appended keywords.
- 0: Business name includes appended keywords like "Buick GMC Dealer Albertville" or "#1 Volume Chevy Dealer." This is a Google policy violation.

### 1.3 Category aligned with OEM directory and website (3 pts)
- 3: OEM dealer directory (e.g., gmc.com/dealers, ford.com/dealers) and the dealer website footer describe the dealership in a way consistent with the primary category.
- 0: Mismatch (OEM directory shows multi-brand, GBP primary category is single-brand only, or vice versa) without justification.

## Dimension 2. Secondary categories — 8 pts

Secondary categories expand eligible query coverage. Dealers consistently under-use them.

### 2.1 Service categories present (3 pts)
- 3: "Auto repair shop," "Car repair and maintenance," "Oil change service," "Brake shop," "Tire shop" added as secondary categories where the dealer offers those services.
- 1: Some service categories present but obvious ones missing.
- 0: No service-related secondary categories.

### 2.2 Parts category (2 pts)
- 2: "Auto parts store" present (dealer has parts counter).
- 0: Missing.

### 2.3 Used and CPO category (2 pts)
- 2: "Used car dealer" present (in addition to primary new-car franchise category) if dealer sells used.
- 1: Used car dealer category missing but dealer sells limited used inventory.
- 0: Missing entirely on a dealership that sells used.

### 2.4 No category bloat or spam (1 pt)
- 1: Categories are all relevant. No off-strategy categories ("Truck rental agency" on a sales rooftop, etc.).
- 0: Spam or off-strategy categories present.

## Dimension 3. NAP consistency — 10 pts

Name, Address, Phone. The classic local SEO trifecta. Inconsistency suppresses local pack rankings.

### 3.1 Business name matches legal entity and website (4 pts)
- 4: GBP name exactly matches the website header, footer, and legal entity.
- 2: Minor variation (e.g., "Howard Bentley Buick-GMC" vs "Howard Bentley Buick GMC").
- 0: Major variation, keyword stuffing, or mismatch with OEM directory.

### 3.2 Address matches website, OEM directory, and major citation sources (3 pts)
- 3: Exact match on GBP, website footer, OEM dealer directory, and the top three citation sources (Yelp, Yellow Pages, BBB or DealerRater for autos).
- 1: One mismatch.
- 0: Two or more mismatches, or any suite-number or street-format inconsistency.

### 3.3 Phone number matches and is a local number (3 pts)
- 3: GBP phone matches website primary phone, is a local number with local area code, and is consistent across OEM directory and citations.
- 2: Mismatch on one citation source.
- 1: Phone is a tracking number on the website but the dealer's actual local number is on GBP (preferred), OR the phone is a tracking number on GBP and the local number is elsewhere (acceptable but flag).
- 0: Phone differs across multiple sources, or phone is a national 800 number with no local presence.

## Dimension 4. Hours of operation — 8 pts

### 4.1 Hours present and accurate (3 pts)
- 3: Sales, service, and parts hours all set in GBP. Match the website.
- 1: Only one department's hours set, or hours present but mismatched with website.
- 0: No hours, or hours obviously stale (Saturday hours show "Closed" but dealer is open Saturday).

### 4.2 Holiday and special hours managed (3 pts)
- 3: Last 6 months show holiday hours set for major holidays (Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, etc.).
- 1: Some holiday hours set but inconsistent.
- 0: No holiday hours ever set. (Google penalizes "Hours may differ" warnings.)

### 4.3 Departments correctly broken out (2 pts)
- 2: Sales department, Service department, and Parts department each have their own hours and (where applicable) own GBP listings linked from the primary.
- 1: One department broken out.
- 0: Single GBP for the entire rooftop with no department breakout, even though the dealer offers all three.

## Dimension 5. Services list — 10 pts

The services list dictates which "near me" service queries the GBP is eligible for. Drastically under-used by dealers.

### 5.1 Sales-floor services populated (3 pts)
- 3: "New car sales," "Used car sales," "Vehicle financing," "Vehicle trade-in evaluation," "Test drives," "Vehicle delivery" populated where applicable.
- 1: Some sales services present.
- 0: No sales services listed.

### 5.2 Service department services populated (4 pts)
- 4: Oil change, brake service, tire rotation, tire replacement, battery replacement, diagnostic check, transmission service, alignment, A/C service, multi-point inspection populated where applicable.
- 2: Some service department services present.
- 0: None populated despite dealer having a service department.

### 5.3 OEM-specific service offerings populated (3 pts)
- 3: OEM-specific services populated (e.g., GM Certified Service, Ford QuickLane, Toyota Express Maintenance, Honda Service Pass, etc.) where the dealer participates in the OEM program.
- 1: OEM program participation visible but not listed in services.
- 0: Missing entirely.

---

## Dimension 6. Attributes — 7 pts

Attributes are GBP-managed flags that surface in Search (wheelchair accessible, Wi-Fi, etc.) and influence eligibility for specific queries.

### 6.1 Accessibility attributes (2 pts)
- 2: Wheelchair-accessible entrance, parking, and restroom flagged where applicable.
- 0: Not set.

### 6.2 Amenity attributes (2 pts)
- 2: Wi-Fi, restrooms, customer lounge, coffee/refreshments where applicable.
- 0: Not set.

### 6.3 Payment attributes (1 pt)
- 1: Credit cards, debit cards, mobile payments, financing accepted where applicable.
- 0: Not set.

### 6.4 Identity attributes (1 pt)
- 1: Where applicable, identity attributes (Veteran-owned, Latino-owned, Black-owned, Women-owned) set per ownership.
- 0: Not set despite applicability.

### 6.5 Service options (1 pt)
- 1: Service options (e.g., "On-site services," "Online appointments," "Same-day appointments") set.
- 0: Not set.

## Dimension 7. Photos and visual freshness — 12 pts

Photos are the single most-engaged-with element on a GBP listing. Recency signals an active dealership.

### 7.1 Cover and profile photo set and current (2 pts)
- 2: Cover photo is a current, high-resolution exterior or interior shot. Profile photo (logo) is the current dealer logo.
- 1: Set but stale (>2 years old or outdated logo).
- 0: Default Google placeholder or missing.

### 7.2 Exterior, interior, team photos uploaded (3 pts)
- 3: Minimum 15 exterior, 15 interior, 5 team photos uploaded. Quality is high. No stock photography.
- 1: Some photos but thin coverage (under 30 total) or visible stock photography.
- 0: Fewer than 10 photos total.

### 7.3 Vehicle inventory photos uploaded (3 pts)
- 3: Dealer regularly uploads vehicle photos. Inventory photos appear in the past 30 days. New arrivals and used inventory both represented.
- 1: Some vehicle photos but no recent uploads.
- 0: No vehicle photos.

### 7.4 Photo recency signal (2 pts)
- 2: At least one photo uploaded by the dealer in the past 30 days.
- 1: Most recent dealer-uploaded photo is 30 to 90 days old.
- 0: Last dealer-uploaded photo is 90+ days old.

### 7.5 Customer photo ratio (2 pts)
- 2: Customer-uploaded photo count is less than 50% of total photo count (dealer is dominant publisher).
- 1: Customer photos roughly equal to dealer photos.
- 0: Customer photos dominate, suggesting dealer is not actively publishing.

## Dimension 8. Posts and engagement — 8 pts

GBP Posts surface in the local pack carousel and signal an active listing.

### 8.1 Post cadence (4 pts)
- 4: A new post published at least every 7 days in the past 90 days. Posts visible in the listing.
- 2: Posts published less than weekly but consistently in the past 90 days.
- 1: Some posts in the past 90 days, gaps visible.
- 0: No posts in the past 90 days.

### 8.2 Post type diversity (2 pts)
- 2: Mix of Offers (incentives, lease specials), Events (test drive events, service specials), and Updates (new arrivals, news).
- 1: Only one post type.
- 0: No posts.

### 8.3 Post content quality (2 pts)
- 2: Posts include high-quality imagery, clear CTAs, and direct URLs to dealer landing pages with UTM tagging.
- 1: Posts present but generic OEM-supplied templates with no dealer customization.
- 0: No posts or broken UTMs.

## Dimension 9. Q&A management — 10 pts

The Q&A section on a GBP is publicly editable. Unanswered questions and customer-supplied answers create misinformation risk and signal an inattentive dealer.

### 9.1 Pre-seeded FAQ present (3 pts)
- 3: Dealer has pre-seeded at least 5 high-intent questions: hours, financing options, brands sold, used inventory availability, service appointment process. Answered by the dealer (verified owner badge).
- 1: Some pre-seeded but thin (under 5).
- 0: No pre-seeded Q&A.

### 9.2 Response rate on customer questions (4 pts)
- 4: 100% of customer-asked questions answered by the dealer (verified owner badge) within 48 hours.
- 2: 50% to 99% answered.
- 1: 25% to 50% answered.
- 0: Less than 25% answered, OR customer-supplied answers dominate.

### 9.3 No incorrect customer answers visible (3 pts)
- 3: Top-voted answers on every question are from the dealer (verified) or are factually correct.
- 1: One incorrect customer answer visible as top result.
- 0: Multiple incorrect customer answers visible as top result.

## Dimension 10. Review signal and response discipline — 15 pts

The largest single dimension. Review signal (count, velocity, quality) and response discipline correlate with local pack rankings and conversion.

### 10.1 Review count vs market (3 pts)
- 3: Review count is at or above the in-market median for same-brand same-size dealers.
- 1: Below median but above 100 total.
- 0: Below 100 total or far below market median.

### 10.2 Review velocity (4 pts)
- 4: At least 8 new reviews per month in the past 6 months. Steady, no gaps.
- 2: 3 to 7 reviews per month.
- 1: 1 to 2 reviews per month.
- 0: Fewer than 1 per month, OR irregular bursts suggesting solicitation campaigns followed by silence.

### 10.3 Average rating (2 pts)
- 2: 4.5 stars or higher.
- 1: 4.0 to 4.4.
- 0: Below 4.0.

### 10.4 Response rate (4 pts)
- 4: 95% or more of reviews have a dealer response.
- 2: 75% to 94%.
- 1: 50% to 74%.
- 0: Under 50%.

### 10.5 Response quality (2 pts)
- 2: Responses are personalized (customer name, vehicle, service department, specific employee). Mix of templates is okay but at least 30% personalized.
- 1: All responses are obvious templates.
- 0: Responses present but contain defensive, argumentative, or unprofessional content.

## Letter grade bands

- 90 to 100: **A.** Top-decile dealer GBP. Local pack contender.
- 80 to 89: **B.** Strong GBP with optimization headroom.
- 70 to 79: **C.** Average dealer GBP. Material local-pack opportunity.
- 60 to 69: **D.** Below average. Significant remediation needed.
- Below 60: **F.** Critical. GBP is actively suppressing local visibility.

## Multi-rooftop dealer group overlay

Apply this overlay AFTER scoring each individual rooftop. The overlay does not change the per-rooftop score; it produces a separate Group Consistency Score on a 0-to-25 scale.

### G1. Naming convention consistency (5 pts)
- 5: All rooftops follow the same naming pattern (e.g., "[Owner Name] [OEM]" consistently).
- 2: Mixed patterns within the group.
- 0: No discernible pattern; ad hoc names per rooftop.

### G2. Primary category consistency (5 pts)
- 5: Primary categories are correct per rooftop's primary OEM. No drift.
- 2: One rooftop has a wrong or generic primary category.
- 0: Multiple rooftops with wrong or generic primary categories.

### G3. Hours format consistency (3 pts)
- 3: Same hours format across all rooftops (e.g., all break out sales, service, parts).
- 1: Format varies but reasonable per rooftop.
- 0: No standard.

### G4. Phone format consistency (2 pts)
- 2: Phone format is consistent (all local numbers OR all tracking numbers, not mixed).
- 0: Mixed.

### G5. Review response standard (5 pts)
- 5: Response rate is above 90% on every rooftop. Tone is consistent (no rooftop is wildly different).
- 2: Response rate varies (some rooftops above 90%, others below).
- 0: Multiple rooftops with response rate under 50%.

### G6. Photo and post cadence standard (3 pts)
- 3: Every rooftop publishes photos and posts on a comparable cadence.
- 1: One rooftop is silent.
- 0: Most rooftops are silent.

### G7. Duplicate listings (2 pts)
- 2: No duplicate GBPs detected.
- 1: One duplicate detected, merge in progress.
- 0: Multiple duplicates.

**Group consistency interpretation:**
- 22 to 25: Group is operating with discipline.
- 17 to 21: Acceptable but inconsistent. Quick wins available.
- 12 to 16: Inconsistency is suppressing market share at the weaker rooftops.
- Below 12: Group has no standard. Highest-leverage remediation in the group.

## House rules for scoring

- When in doubt, score the lower band. This audit is more useful when it is honest than when it is generous.
- A check that cannot be verified from available access is scored Fail. Flag as "verification required" in the report.
- Do not score a dimension as Pass on the strength of one strong sub-check if other sub-checks are weak. Use the per-check sub-scoring, then sum honestly.
- Compute the total to a whole number. Round 0.5 up.
- For multi-rooftop groups, present per-rooftop scores AND the group consistency overlay separately. Do not average rooftop scores; they describe different units of analysis.
