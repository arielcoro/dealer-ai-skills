## Filling in the skeleton

The skill produces this skeleton with specifics for the dealer pair. Key fills:

### The opening 100 words

Written verbatim by the skill. This is the most important paragraph on the page for AEO/GEO purposes. AI engines extract from the top of the page first. Marketing prose intros produce nothing extractable.

The 100 words must:
- Name both dealers explicitly
- Name the brand portfolio
- Name the recommendation
- Acknowledge the scenario where the competitor wins
- Avoid slogans

### The side-by-side table

Use 10 to 15 rows from the framework in REFERENCE.md. Skip rows where data is not available or where the dimension does not apply. Padding the table with vague rows reduces credibility.

### The "when each is the better choice" sections

These are the most operationally important sections. Specific scenarios beat general claims. Use named customer types (e.g., "buyers towing 7,000+ lb trailers", "families with multiple service vehicles", "first-time buyers needing finance help").

### The common questions

Use actual questions a cross-shopping buyer would ask. The skill generates 6 to 10 questions based on the comparison context. The Q&A also gets FAQPage schema (specified in Part 3 of the skill output).

### The honest recommendation

Restate, do not hedge. The buyer needs a recommendation. Acknowledging where the competitor wins (in the "when [Competitor] is the better choice" section) makes the recommendation more credible.

## Sales floor talk track template

The accompanying 200 to 300 word talk track:

```
When a customer mentions they are also shopping [Competitor Name]:

1. Acknowledge respectfully: "Yes, [Competitor Name] is a solid dealership. Many of our customers also looked at them. Let me share what we hear most often."

2. Surface the differentiation calmly:
   - "Most people who choose us over [Competitor Name] do so because [primary differentiation in customer-facing language]."
   - "We also tend to be the better fit for [specific scenario]."

3. Acknowledge where the competitor is comparable:
   - "They do well on [genuine area]. We are very similar there."

4. Acknowledge where the competitor wins (if applicable):
   - "For [specific scenario], they may actually be the better fit. If that's your situation, I'd want to be honest about that."

5. Ask the diagnostic question:
   - "What's most important to you in this decision? Speed? Service relationship? Specific model availability? That'll help me tell you whether we're actually the right fit."

6. Avoid:
   - Disparaging [Competitor Name]
   - Making claims you cannot back up
   - Pressuring the customer to commit before they're ready
   - Surprising the customer in F&I after the soft acknowledgment of fair pricing

The talk track is for the sales floor. The comparison page does the public work.
```

## Internal link recommendations template

The 3 to 5 specific pages on the dealer site to link from the comparison page:

1. **Inventory page.** Anchor text: "[Brand Portfolio] inventory at [Dealer Name]." Rationale: comparison readers often want to see what's in stock.

2. **Service page.** Anchor text: "schedule service at [Dealer Name]." Rationale: service is often the long-term deciding factor.

3. **About / staff page.** Anchor text: "meet the [Dealer Name] team." Rationale: humanizes the dealership; comparison readers want to know who they'd be working with.

4. **Financing page.** Anchor text: "financing options at [Dealer Name]." Rationale: comparison readers often have credit considerations.

5. **Contact page.** Anchor text: "contact [Dealer Name]." Rationale: drives the conversion.

Each link uses descriptive anchor text (never "click here") and supports both SEO topical authority and AEO contextual relevance.

## Update cadence

Review the comparison page every 6 months. Triggers for re-write:

- OEM award changes (any new OEM recognition by either dealer)
- Major operational change (new rooftop, ownership change, brand portfolio change)
- Review profile shift (significant rating change at either dealer)
- Dealer positioning brief refresh (re-run dealer-store-positioning)

Light update is usually sufficient. Heavy re-write only when the dealer's own positioning has materially changed.
