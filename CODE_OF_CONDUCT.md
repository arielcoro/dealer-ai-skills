# Code of Conduct

The Dealer AI Skills marketplace is an open-source project that welcomes contributions from dealer principals, agency operators, OEM marketing leaders, developers, and anyone with relevant industry experience. This Code of Conduct exists to ensure the project remains a productive and respectful space for all contributors.

## Standards

Contributors are expected to:

- **Be respectful.** Treat other contributors as colleagues. Disagree on substance, never on character.
- **Be honest.** Cite sources. Distinguish opinion from fact. Acknowledge what you do not know.
- **Be vendor-neutral.** The marketplace serves all dealers regardless of platform, CRM, DMS, OEM, or vendor relationship. Promotion of specific vendors, products, or paid services is not welcome.
- **Disclose conflicts of interest.** If you are affiliated with a vendor named in a skill, disclose that affiliation in your contribution. If you are submitting a contribution that benefits your employer, disclose that too.
- **Respect confidentiality.** Do not submit information confidential to any third party (dealer client, employer, vendor, customer). The marketplace contains no client-specific information by design.
- **Use inclusive language.** Treat all dealer professionals, regardless of language, background, role, or experience, as full participants.
- **Receive feedback gracefully.** Reviewers may request changes, edits, or rewrites. Engage constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behaviors will result in declined contributions or contributor removal:

- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment of any kind, in any channel related to the project.
- Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, national origin, language, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic.
- Doxxing or sharing private information about any contributor, dealer, or vendor without consent.
- Spam, vendor promotion disguised as contribution, or use of the project for unsolicited marketing.
- Submitting work that is not yours or that violates a confidentiality or IP agreement.
- Repeated submission of contributions previously declined for substantive reasons without addressing the original feedback.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers (Ariel Coro and any designated co-maintainers) are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. Reports of violations should be sent to [ariel@tutecnologia.com](mailto:ariel@tutecnologia.com). Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.

Maintainers may, at their discretion:

- Issue a private or public warning.
- Decline a specific contribution.
- Block a contributor from the repository.
- Refer egregious violations to relevant authorities or industry bodies.

Maintainers' decisions on Code of Conduct matters are final.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and comments.
- Email correspondence about the project.
- Public communications referencing the project (Twitter, LinkedIn, podcasts, conferences).
- Any in-person or virtual event organized around the project.
