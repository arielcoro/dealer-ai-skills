From website evidence to actionable fixes

Website URL + goals → Representative page evidence → Readiness assessment → Prioritized fix plan

What it assesses

  • Search discovery and access: directives, links, responses, rendering, and observed access barriers.
  • Answerable content: clear offerings, decision questions, consistency and freshness.
  • Identity and data integrity: attributable claims and accurate machine-readable representations.
  • Customer handoffs: next actions, contact destinations, disclosures and usability.
  • Measurement and maintenance: available evidence of tracking and content updates.

Optional connected-assistant and MCP readiness is reviewed separately, not treated as a requirement for AI search visibility.

What you receive

A dated evidence ledger, category scores with coverage and unknowns, a provisional score range where necessary, and fixes with owners and acceptance tests. AI-answer tests and same-scenario competitor comparisons are included only when access and scope allow them.

How do I use it?

Download the ZIP and upload it through Claude's skill management interface where supported. Alternatively, add the GitHub marketplace in Claude Code and install:

/plugin marketplace add arielcoro/dealer-ai-skills
/plugin install website-ai-readiness@dealer-ai-skills

Then ask: “Assess this website's AI readiness: [URL]. Our main customer action is [goal]. Give me evidence-backed findings and prioritized fixes.”

Is this an automatic website scanner?

No. It is an instruction-based assessment workflow. Claude needs appropriate web tools or supplied artifacts. Without live access, it reports a provisional artifact-based review rather than inventing results.

Does it change my website or submit forms?

No. Assessment does not authorize edits, bookings, lead submissions, purchases, or private-system access.

Does a high score guarantee AI recommendations?

No. Search access, training policies, and connected assistants are different. Missing llms.txt or MCP alone does not lower the score, and no file or markup guarantees citations, traffic, or sales.
