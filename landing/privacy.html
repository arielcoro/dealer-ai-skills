Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026.

This privacy policy explains what information dealeraiskills.com collects, how it is used, and what your rights are. We have written it in plain language because most privacy policies are unreadable and that is bad for everyone.

Dealeraiskills.com is published by Ariel Coro, operating as Tu Tecnologia LLC (doing business as Dealer Growth Hackers and Dealer AI Guy). Contact: ariel@tutecnologia.com.

What we collect

This site is a static landing page. We do not have forms. We do not ask you to sign up. We do not gate any content behind email capture. There is no account system.

The information that is collected is limited to the following:

  • Anonymized analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is privacy-preserving and does not use cookies. It records aggregate page-view counts, referrers, country-level location, and browser type. It does not record your IP address in a way we can read or identify you with.
  • Standard server logs. Cloudflare Pages records standard web server access logs (IP address, requested URL, user-agent, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention. These logs are retained per Cloudflare's policy and are not used by us for tracking or marketing.

We do not run advertising trackers. We do not use third-party cookies. We do not sell or share data with brokers.

Third parties you may interact with from this site

Links on dealeraiskills.com may direct you to third-party properties that have their own privacy policies. The main ones:

  • GitHub when you visit the marketplace repo at github.com/arielcoro/dealer-ai-skills or install a skill. GitHub's privacy policy applies once you leave dealeraiskills.com.
  • Dealer AI Guy at dealeraiguy.com, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Dealer Growth Hackers at dealergrowthhackers.com, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Your AI agent vendor (Anthropic for Claude Code, OpenAI for Codex, etc.) when you install or run a skill. Their privacy policies govern what happens inside the agent. We do not have visibility into agent sessions.

What happens when you install a skill

Skills published in this marketplace are MIT-licensed open source. When you install one, it is copied into your local AI agent environment (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, or similar). The skill runs entirely on your side. We do not receive your conversation, your data, your audit answers, or your scores.

If you use a skill to audit a dealership and the skill produces a report, that report exists only inside your AI agent session. We do not see it. We do not store it. If you choose to share a result with us by emailing it, then it becomes covered by the section below.

If you contact us by email

If you email ariel@tutecnologia.com or any other Dealer AI Guy or Dealer Growth Hackers address, we receive your email content, your email address, and any attachments you send. We use that information only to respond to you and follow up on the inquiry. We do not add you to a marketing list without your explicit consent.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under privacy laws like GDPR, CCPA, or state-specific laws to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you.

Because we do not maintain an account database for this site and the analytics we use are anonymized, in most cases we do not hold personal information about you. If you have specifically corresponded with us by email and want your correspondence deleted, email ariel@tutecnologia.com and we will honor the request within 30 days.

Security

Dealeraiskills.com is served from Cloudflare with TLS 1.3 encryption. The GitHub repo is public by design. The skills themselves are open source.

We follow reasonable practices to secure any email correspondence you send us. We are not a financial institution and do not handle customer financial information through this property. If you handle dealer customer information using one of our skills inside your own AI agent, you are responsible for complying with the FTC Safeguards Rule and any other applicable regulations on that data.

Changes

If we change this policy, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material changes will also be flagged on the landing page for a reasonable period.
